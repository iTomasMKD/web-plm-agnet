Questions, answered

 

What does Agentra do?

Agentra integrates AI across the product lifecycle by connecting data resources, business systems and machines. It also provides infrastructure to build, test, deploy, monitor and retire AI agents, with versions, approvals and audit history in one place.

Why manage AI agents as part of PLM?

PLM connects agents to product information and business processes. Managing AI initiatives there helps teams clarify ownership, coordinate implementation and approvals, and follow an agent from pilot into operation.

Where can I run my AI agents?

On Azure or AWS, inside your own infrastructure, or on a personal server. The same agent definition works in all three.

Which industries does the platform support?

Manufacturing, automotive, energy and utilities, finance, healthcare, and automation and robotics.

Is it ready for security and compliance reviews?

Yes. Access control, audit trails and approval gates are built in, so reviewers can see every change.
